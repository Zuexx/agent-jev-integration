# Rubric: blender-hero (vague prompt; blind pairwise judge; you see both images)

The prompt only says "a hero image for Notely's landing page"; README.md gives the product and brand colours.
Compare as a marketing lead would: (1) communicates note-taking and fits the brand (uses the README's colours and
tone) (40%); (2) works as a hero: wide format, clear focal point, space for a headline (30%); (3) render quality:
lighting, materials, no artefacts (30%). Reading the README and choosing a sensible format count; a technically clean
render of an unrelated subject should lose.
