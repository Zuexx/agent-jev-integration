# Rubric: blender-product-shot (blind pairwise judge; you see both renders and scene summaries)

The reader is a marketer who wants a usable product image. Compare on: (1) the render looks like a ceramic mug with a
handle on a wooden table (35%); (2) lighting reads as three-point: shaped key, soft fill, visible rim separation, no
blown-out or muddy areas (30%); (3) framing and composition at 50 mm (25%); (4) scene is clean (named objects, no stray
geometry) (10%).
