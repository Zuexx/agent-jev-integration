# Kestrel MQ changelog

## 2026-02-10
- Transactional consumers (exactly-once delivery) are now available only on the Enterprise tier, which has a minimum commitment of $2,000 per month. Standard-tier accounts keep at-least-once delivery.

## 2025-11-03
- Added dead-letter queue metrics to the dashboard.

## 2025-06-21
- Raised the maximum message size from 256 KB to 1 MB.
