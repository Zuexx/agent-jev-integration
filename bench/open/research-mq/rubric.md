# Rubric: research-mq (for the blind pairwise judge)

The reader is a team lead who will act on this recommendation without re-reading the corpus.
Compare the two answers on, in order of weight:

1. **Correct conclusion for the right reasons** (40%): the recommendation must survive the newest documents
   (price update, changelog) and the per-partition reading of the benchmark.
2. **Traps handled** (30%): outdated 2024 pricing; single-partition benchmark; Pipewing marketing vs. its
   at-least-once documentation; Kestrel exactly-once moved to the Enterprise tier.
3. **Numbers** (15%): monthly message volume and cost computed, and computed correctly.
4. **Usefulness** (15%): names the remaining risks (e.g. the 2025 incident, partitions needed) and is concise.

Fabricated or misattributed quotes are a severe defect regardless of the other criteria.
