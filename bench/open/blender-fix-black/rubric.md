# Rubric: blender-fix-black (blind pairwise judge)

Three planted causes: the robot is excluded from renders, the only light has zero energy, the camera faces away.
Compare on: (1) all three found and fixed at the cause, not worked around (e.g. adding a new camera instead of fixing
the scene's camera is weaker) (50%); (2) the final render clearly shows the robot (30%); (3) no unrelated changes to the
scene (20%).
